# Product

## Register
product

## Users
Students, academics, and researchers who need a quick, private, and offline way to manipulate research papers, assignments, and documents without uploading sensitive or large files to the cloud.

## Product Purpose
To provide a fast, offline, and secure utility suite for PDF and document manipulation. Success looks like users accomplishing their document conversion or merging tasks in seconds without friction.

## Brand Personality
Clean, modern, trustworthy, and incredibly fast. It should feel like a native, robust academic tool rather than a flashy marketing site.

## Anti-references
- Bloated SaaS dashboards with unnecessary charts or widgets
- "Playful" or overly whimsical startup aesthetics
- Sluggish, heavy client-side rendering
- Generic "glassmorphism" that distracts from the core utility

## Design Principles
- **Utilitarian Speed**: The interface should be aggressively fast and responsive.
- **Academic Trust**: Use clean typography and structured layouts that inspire confidence and look professional.
- **Clarity Over Cleverness**: Labels and buttons should be unambiguous. Actions must be immediately obvious.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast for readability (WCAG AA).
- Clear, descriptive focus states for keyboard navigation.
- Accessible error messages and loading states.
